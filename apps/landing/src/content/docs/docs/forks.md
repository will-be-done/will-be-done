I welcome forks of Will Be Done and appreciate everyone who takes the time to
make it their own. Change the interface, try a different workflow, or build
something for your own needs. Here are some projects worth a look.

## best-open-sunsama

![The best-open-sunsama Today view, with a task list beside an hourly calendar.](/forks/best-open-sunsama-today.png)

[best-open-sunsama](https://github.com/josericardopenase/best-open-sunsama/tree/main)
by [@josericardopenase](https://github.com/josericardopenase) is a Sunsama-inspired
fork focused on daily planning. It builds on Will Be Done's local-first core and
adds:

- A Today list alongside a time grid, with planned durations and start times.
- A weekly calendar with workday hours and breaks.
- A Pomodoro timer linked to tasks.
- End-of-day reports with completed tasks, notes, and ratings for mood, energy,
  focus, and accomplishment.

See the [fork's README](https://github.com/josericardopenase/best-open-sunsama#readme)
for screenshots and instructions for running its version of the app.

## Make your own

If you want to take Will Be Done in another direction,
[fork the repository](https://github.com/will-be-done/will-be-done/fork).
The [development guide](https://github.com/will-be-done/will-be-done#development)
explains how to run it locally.
