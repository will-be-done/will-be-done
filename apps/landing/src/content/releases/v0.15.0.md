See your meetings next to your tasks with read-only CalDAV and iCal calendars, scroll the timeline through any week, and manage a space from full settings pages.

## What's new

### Calendar events

Connect your calendars to see their events alongside your tasks. Open **Space settings → Calendars** and add either:

- a **CalDAV account**: the server address, your username, and an app password. iCloud, Fastmail, and Nextcloud work this way. Untick the calendars you don't want to see.
- a **calendar link**: an `.ics` or `webcal://` address, such as Google Calendar's "Secret address in iCal format" or a published Outlook calendar.

Events appear in every Calendar view, at the top of each day on the timeline, and in Today. Timed events show in your device's time zone, and repeating events show on every day they occur. Click an event to see its description, guests, and a **Join meeting** button for Zoom, Meet, Teams, and similar links.

- Events are read-only. Change them in the calendar app they come from.
- Invitations you declined are hidden. Events you haven't answered or marked as maybe are striped, and events marked as free are shown as an outline.
- The server refreshes events every 15 minutes, and **Refresh** in settings fetches them right away.
- Past events are kept for one month by default. Choose from 2 weeks up to **All events** under each calendar.
- Once synced, events are available offline. Removing a calendar removes its events from every device.

### Endless timeline

The timeline no longer stops at the edge of the week. Scroll sideways to move through days in either direction without end.

- The arrows next to the date move one day, **Today** returns to the current week, and clicking the date jumps to any day.
- Hold a dragged task near the left or right edge to scroll to days further away.
- The day you are looking at is kept in the address bar, so links and the browser's back button return to the same place.
- In the projects panel, tasks scheduled on a day currently on screen have a dashed outline.

### Settings pages

Space settings moved from a popup to full pages with their own sidebar: General, Account, API tokens, Backup, Import, and Calendars. Each page has its own address, so you can link to it and use the back button.

### Smaller improvements

- Hover **maps** in the navigation to open one of your five most recently opened mindmaps.
- Lists, boards, dialogs, and calendar cells have the same slim scrollbars on every platform.
- On a touch screen, press and hold a calendar block briefly to drag it.
- In an empty column, the column itself takes focus. Press `i`, `o`, or `a` there to add a task.

## Fixes

- Changes downloaded from the server now reach every open tab, not only the one doing the sync, and the live connection to the server reconnects after it drops.
- Devices that edited the same item while offline now always settle on the same result, whatever order their changes arrive in. A later edit is no longer lost to a default value from another device.
- Recurring tasks no longer cause repeated background sync activity.

## Upgrade notes

- The sync protocol changed. Once the server is updated, tabs that were already open show **Update required** in the sync indicator until you reload them. Existing data needs no manual migration.
- Self-hosted installations: pull `ghcr.io/will-be-done/will-be-done:0.15.0` (or `latest`) and recreate the container.
- Self-hosted installations: calendar connections stay off until you set `WBD_CALENDAR_ENCRYPTION_KEY`. Generate a key once, for example with `openssl rand -base64 48`, and keep it stable. To reach a CalDAV server on your own network, such as Radicale or Nextcloud, also set `WBD_CALENDAR_ALLOW_PRIVATE_NETWORKS=true`. See [Self-hosting](https://will-be-done.app/docs/self-hosting/#calendar-connections) for details.
