## What's new

### Google sign-in and account settings

- Sign in with Google on web and desktop.
- Connect or disconnect Google from an existing account.
- Verify your email address and recover a forgotten password by email.
- Change your account email address from Account settings.

### Sync status

A new sidebar indicator shows whether your space is syncing, up to date, offline, or interrupted. Open it to see transfer progress and the last successful sync time.

This release also fixes syncing changes made in other browser tabs.

### Everyday improvements

- Press `?` to open the keyboard shortcuts reference.
- Open links directly from task text.
- Drag tasks onto Inbox in the sidebar to move them there.
- Newly created projects open automatically.

## Fixes

- Task titles no longer flicker after editing.
- Moving a task to another project preserves keyboard focus on a nearby task.
- Keyboard navigation handles empty and closed stashes correctly.
- The date picker stays open during rapid month navigation.
- Daily dates and overdue counts handle time zones correctly.
- Drag indicators match valid drop targets and clear when dragging away from Inbox.
- Google sign-in returns to the app on the domain where sign-in started.

## Other changes

- Expanded documentation for installation, self-hosting, the API, keyboard shortcuts, and offline use.
- Added a website features page, clearer alpha pricing, and improved mobile navigation.
- Reworked local database preloading and improved server transaction handling.
- Added product analytics and error reporting.
- Made the server's global rate limit configurable.

## Self-hosting notes

Google sign-in and account email delivery are optional and require server configuration. Existing email-and-password sign-in remains available without them.

See the [Google sign-in setup guide](https://github.com/will-be-done/will-be-done/blob/v0.12.0/docs/google-auth.md) for OAuth and related email configuration.
